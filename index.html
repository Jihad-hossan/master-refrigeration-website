About

MD. M. Babu: technician, trainer, entrepreneur

After studying at Chapainawabganj Govt. Polytechnic Institute, he built his career in refrigeration and air conditioning. Today he is the owner and head of service at Master Refrigeration, a trainer at Technical Training Centre, Pabna, and a CBT&A certified trainer and assessor under the National Skills Development Authority (NSDA).

His belief is simple: skill is what makes a person self-reliant and respected. That is why he serves customers on one side and builds new technicians on the other.
